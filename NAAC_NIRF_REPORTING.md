# ProofHire 2.0 – NAAC / NIRF Institutional Reporting

`AccreditationReportService` compiles traceable snapshots (`institutional_reports`) from authorized operational records:
- Displays reporting period, metric sources, missing-data warnings, generating user, timestamp, and `audit_log` reference IDs.
- Supports **CSV** (`/api/intelligence/reports/{id}/export-csv`) and **PDF** (`/api/intelligence/reports/{id}/export-pdf`) exports.
- **Statutory Disclaimer:** ProofHire is not officially certified or endorsed by NAAC or NIRF, and never invents NAAC/NIRF grades, criteria scores, or institutional rankings.
